Types of Services

MotoFix is a digital-first motorcycle shop management system engineered to streamline vehicle maintenance, parts retail, and shop operations.

Engine Overhaul

Complete cylinder head and block restoration, Includes deep-cleaning components, replacing all standard gaskets, valves, piston rings, and executing blueprint clearance checks.

Electrical Diagnostics

Full-spectrum mapping of wire harness resistance, stator output verification, battery load profiling, ECU diagnostic readouts, and complete sensor-by-sensor fault tracing.

Suspension Calibration

Complete front fork and rear shock disassembly includes cartridge cleaning, seals and dust wipers renewal, fresh fluid flush tuned to rider weight, and sag setting.

Performance Exhaust

Integration of aftermarket high-flow header pipes. and slip-on exhaust mufflers. Includes oxygen sensor relocation, exhaust flange sealing, and gas leakage integrity testing.

Chain & Drive Kit

Comprehensive driveline renewal. Features dual sprocket extraction (hardened), high-tensile O-ring chain installation, precise alignment setup, and laser slack tensioning.

Fuel Injector Tuning

Ultrasonic cleaning of fuel injector nozzles, precise flow-bench pattern verification, fuel rail pressure testing, and custom map calibration on the throttle body assembly.

Basic Oil Change

Replace the engine oil and filter to protect internal parts and keep the motorcycle running smoothly. Service takes about 45 minutes.

Full Tune-Up

Replace spark plugs, Inspect the air filter, clean the carburetor, and adjust the chain for smoother performance. Service takes about two hours.

Brake System Service

Service the brake pads, flush the fluid, and inspect the rotors to restore reliable stopping power. Service takes about 1.5 hours.

Tire Replacement

Mount and balance a front or rear tire to improve handling and rider safety. Service takes about one hour

Parts Service

MotoFix bridges the gap between digital convenience and hands-on mechanical service through an on-site parts workflow:

Online Parts Browsing

Customers can check the shop's inventory online before visiting, allowing them to see if specific replacement parts or custom components are currently in stock.

Service Appointment Bundling

When booking a maintenance slot, customers can attach desired parts requests to their appointment so the shop prepares them ahead of their visit.

In-Shop Diagnosis & Add-ons

If a mechanic identifies a broken or worn-out part during the physical inspection, they can log the required replacement part directly into the active repair ticket.

In-Person Installation & Settlement

Because MotoFix operates as a physical service center, there is no home delivery. All parts are claimed, professionally installed by mechanics, and settled directly at the shop counter.
